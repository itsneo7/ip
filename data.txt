Honeybees are tiny flying insects that play a massive role in keeping our natural world alive and healthy. These social creatures live together in large groups called colonies, where every bee has a specific job like gathering food, building the hive, or taking care of the baby bees. As they fly from flower to flower to collect sweet nectar, yellow dust called pollen sticks to their fuzzy bodies and gets carried to other plants. This important movement helps flowers and food crops grow fruits, vegetables, and seeds. Without the hard work of these small animals, our gardens and farms would produce much less food for people and animals to eat.
